# Embryo Breadboard

Embryo Breadboard is Embryo-Zero's fork of [fritzing-app](https://github.com/fritzing/fritzing-app) (5aa56a5, commit 5aa56a5). It does breadboard sketches for beginners.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE.CC-BY-SA, LICENSE.GPL2, LICENSE.GPL3, LICENSE.LGPLv3, LICENSE.Modified_BSD, LICENSE_1_0.txt.BOOST, license-openssl-ssleay.txt) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
